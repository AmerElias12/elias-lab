Research Overview

A New Route into the Genome

Replacing break-and-repair editing with precise, programmable integration at the genome's own landing sites.

The Challenge

Current Limitations in Genome Editing

Traditional genome-editing tools like CRISPR, ZFNs, and TALENs create double-strand breaks (DSBs) to induce gene editing. However, they struggle with inserting large DNA sequences at precise genomic locations and often generate unintended modifications including indels, off-target effects, and oncogene activation.

Our Solution

Tyrosine Site-Specific Integrases

We are developing engineered tyrosine recombinases—particularly HK022—that recognize and integrate into native genomic attachment sites (attB) without creating double-strand breaks. Our discovery identified approximately 40,000 unique native attB sites across the human genome, enabling precise, large-scale DNA integration for therapeutic applications.

Key Advantages

  • No Double-Strand Breaks: Eliminates risks of mosaicism and unintended modifications
  • Large DNA Payloads: Enables integration of multi-kilobase sequences efficiently
  • Mutation-Independent: Single therapy can treat patients with different mutations in the same gene
  • High Specificity: Targets native genomic sites with minimal off-target effects
  • Multiplexing Capability: Multiple genetic payloads can be integrated simultaneously at distinct loci
What We're Building

Research Aims & Objectives

1

Discovery & Engineering

Discover and engineer novel tyrosine recombinase variants through metagenomic analysis, phage-assisted continuous evolution (PACE), and AlphaFold3-guided protein engineering to optimize specificity for native human genomic sites.

2

HK022 Optimization

Engineer HK022 integrase through directed and random mutagenesis to develop highly active variants with minimal off-target effects. Demonstrate proof-of-concept across three therapeutic models.

3

Genomic Safety

Establish comprehensive safety profiles through whole-genome next-generation sequencing to detect and characterize unintended genomic changes and off-target integrations.

4

CRISPR-Recombinase Fusion

Design and optimize chimeric proteins fusing CRISPR-dCas variants with tyrosine recombinases to enhance targeting efficiency and enable multiplexed genome engineering.

5

Multiplexed Integration

Demonstrate simultaneous integration of multiple distinct genetic payloads at different genomic loci in a single transfection event for complex gene circuit engineering.

6

Delivery Optimization

Evaluate clinical delivery systems including lipid nanoparticles, exosomes, and AAV vectors for efficient and safe in vivo therapeutic payload integration.

Where It Leads

Therapeutic Applications

One precise-integration platform, applied across loss-of-function disease, cancer, and complex cell engineering.

Safe-Harbor Integration

Stable Transgene Expression

Targeting well-characterized safe loci (Rosa26, CLYBL, H11) for stable, predictable gene expression in therapeutic applications. Ideal for restoring gene function in loss-of-function diseases.

Promoter-Trap Therapy

Alpha-1 Antitrypsin Deficiency (AATD)

Inserting SERPINA1 cDNA at the APLP2 locus to leverage native promoters for disease-independent therapy. A single treatment applicable to all AATD patients regardless of their specific mutation.

RMCE Technology

Tumor Suppressor Gene Correction

Precise replacement of mutated BRCA1 and TP53 sequences with healthy wild-type donor DNA. Enables "pan-mutation" therapeutics that correct disease-causing variants across patient populations.

Neurodevelopmental Disorders

Haploinsufficiency Correction

Targeting genes like GRIN2B and SHANK3 to restore normal gene dosage in autism spectrum disorders and other neurodevelopmental conditions through safe-harbor integration.

Cancer Immunotherapy

Enhanced CAR-T Engineering

Multiplexed integration of multi-gene constructs for advanced immunotherapies. Enables complex gene circuit installation for more effective cancer immunotherapy production.

Genetic Disease

Cystic Fibrosis & Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy

Large-scale DNA insertion approaches for diseases caused by heterogeneous mutations. One therapeutic approach beneficial to patients with diverse mutations in the same gene.

How We Work

Methodology & Technical Approach

🧬 Metagenomic Discovery & Bioinformatic Mining

Comprehensive screening of prokaryotic and archaeal databases to identify candidate tyrosine recombinase subtypes containing the conserved R-H-R-Y catalytic tetrad. Bioinformatic analysis identifies native attB sites within human genomic safe harbors and disease-associated genes.

🔬 Phage-Assisted Continuous Evolution (PACE)

High-throughput directed evolution using progressively shorter landing pads (34 bp → <25 bp) as selection pressure. Generates one-directional recombinase variants with improved activity and genome targeting specificity.

🔍 AlphaFold3-Guided Protein Engineering

Structural modeling identifies mutational hotspots at the protein-DNA interface. Strategic stacking of mutations enhances specificity for native human recombination sites and optimizes catalytic efficiency.

🧪 Directed & Random Mutagenesis

Molecular evolution of HK022 integrase through systematic and random mutagenesis to discover variants with enhanced activity, reduced off-target binding, and improved tolerance for diverse genomic targets.

⚡ Fluorescent Promoter-Trap Reporter Assays

Functional validation in human cells (HEK293) using GFP/mCherry/BFP reporters. Quantifies integration efficiency and measures off-target activity through FACS, qRT-PCR, NGS, and immunostaining.

📊 Whole-Genome Next-Generation Sequencing

Unbiased genomic profiling through bulk and single-cell NGS of recombination junctions. Comprehensive analysis of off-target integrations and chromosomal alterations to establish safety profiles.

🔗 CRISPR-dCas & Recombinase Fusion Engineering

Design of chimeric proteins combining programmable DNA-binding (CRISPR-dCas9, CasMINI, AsCas12f) with tyrosine recombinase catalytic domains. Flexible linker optimization and dual-targeting capability testing.

🚚 Delivery System Evaluation

Assessment of clinical delivery platforms: lipid nanoparticles (LNPs), exosomes, and adeno-associated viruses (AAV). Evaluation of efficiency, safety, and in vivo integration outcomes across delivery systems.
